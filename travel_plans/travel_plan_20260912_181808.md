## 6️⃣ Health & Safety Tips

1. **Acclimatization** – Spend at least one night at **Gopeshwar** (2,400 m) before starting the trek.  
2. **Hydration** – Drink 2‑3 L of water daily; add electrolytes if you sweat a lot.  
3. **Altitude** – Rudranath sits at ~3,600 m. If you feel headache, nausea, or dizziness, descend immediately.  
4. **Foot Care** – Apply moleskin on hot spots; change socks at every rest stop.  
5. **Travel Insurance** – Choose a policy covering high‑altitude trekking and emergency evacuation.  
6. **Respect Local Customs** – The Rudranath Temple is a sacred site; dress modestly (long sleeves, long trousers) and follow the priest’s instructions.
